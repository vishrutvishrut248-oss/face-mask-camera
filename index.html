ℹ️ About & rights

Face Mask Camera — a Snapchat-style live filter that runs entirely in your browser: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (blendshapes + face transformation matrix) drives a Three.js face mesh textured with your uploaded image, mapped through the canonical face-mesh UVs. The camera feed never leaves your device; there is no backend and no account.

⚖️ Copyright notice: you may only upload images you created or have the rights or permission to use. Masks you save are stored in this browser's local storage only. If you share photos or recordings, make sure you also have the rights to the mask artwork used in them.

Tips: use ⚡ Lite if the preview lags (drops camera resolution and detects every 2nd frame to hold 30 fps). 🎯 Calibrate re-opens the 6-point alignment at any time.

⚖️ Before you upload

Only use images you created or have the rights / permission to use. Fan art and characters may be copyrighted — personal, private use only; don't redistribute what you don't own. Your image is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
